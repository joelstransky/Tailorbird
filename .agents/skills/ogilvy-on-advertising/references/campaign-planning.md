# Ogilvy Campaign Planning & Media Strategy Reference

> *"Never stop testing, and your advertising will never stop improving."* — David Ogilvy, *Ogilvy on Advertising*

This document provides guidelines for planning, structuring, and executing marketing campaigns and ad budget allocation based on David Ogilvy's principles of media efficiency and rigorous testing.

---

## 1. Audience-First Media Selection

Ad spend is wasted when placed in channels where your ideal buyers are not actively engaged or receptive.

1. **Profile the True Buyer**: Identify where your highest-LTV (Lifetime Value) customers consume information and make purchasing decisions.
2. **Intent vs. Interruption**:
   - **High-Intent Channels (Search, Marketplace, Direct Inquiry)**: Capture buyers who are actively searching for solutions. Allocate budget here first to secure baseline conversions.
   - **Interruption/Discovery Channels (Social Video, Display, Native)**: Educate potential buyers who aren't actively searching yet. Use editorial-style storytelling and clear problem-solution angles.
   - **Direct Retention Channels (Email, SMS, Direct Mail)**: High-ROI channels for repeat purchases and customer retention.

---

## 2. The Testing & Rollout Framework

Never commit your full campaign budget upfront to an unproven concept or channel.

### The 3-Step Campaign Rollout
1. **Pilot / Test Phase (10-15% of Budget)**:
   - Test multiple headline variations, core offers, and creative angles in small, controlled audience segments.
   - Measure actual cost-per-acquisition (CPA) or direct response metrics—not vanity impressions or clicks.
2. **Optimization Phase (20-30% of Budget)**:
   - Double down on the winning headline, copy, and channel combinations.
   - Refine landing pages, email follow-up sequences, and checkout conversion paths.
3. **Scale / Heavy-Up Phase (60-70% of Budget)**:
   - Roll out the proven campaign to broader audiences and primary media channels.

---

## 3. Budget Allocation & Concentration

Avoid the trap of spreading a limited budget thinly across 10 different platforms.

- **Rule of Concentration**: It is far more effective to dominate 1 or 2 primary channels where your audience lives than to have an invisible, weak presence on every platform.
- **Budget Priority Order**:
  1. Direct Response / Retargeting / High-Intent Search (Immediate ROI)
  2. Core Prospecting Channels (Proven high-LTV channel for your specific niche)
  3. Experimental Channels (Allocated only after core channels are optimized)

---

## 4. Message-Market Fit by Channel

Match the presentation style to the media environment:

| Channel | Recommended Format / Angle | Key Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Search (Google/Bing)** | Factual, benefit-led headline matching exact search intent | Conversion Rate & CPA |
| **B2B / Professional (LinkedIn, Email)** | Case study, whitepaper, editorial report, long-copy article | Qualified Leads & Booked Demos |
| **Social / Video (Meta, YouTube, TikTok)** | Hook within 3 seconds, problem-solution narrative, clear CTA | ROAS & Cost Per Sale |
| **Direct Mail / Email Newsletter** | Long personal letter format, specific facts, exclusive offer | Direct Revenue per Email/Mailed Piece |
